Founders Story | Mr. Lee and Nancy Jan
Kaigui Ornament Co., Ltd. was founded Mr. Lee and Nancy Jan.
Nancy’s journey in foreign trade and the gift industry began in her
twenties. Starting as a salesperson, she learned the business step by step
through hands-on experience, continuous learning, and years of
participation in trade shows and customer development.
Although Nancy did not begin with a strong English foundation, she was
never afraid to move forward. She has always believed that if something
is right, it is worth to stay committed and keep going. With
determination, courage, and years of industry experience, she gradually
built the foundation of what Kaigui is today.
Another key figure behind Kaigui’s development is Mr. Lee, Nancy’s
husband, who played an important role in the company’s early growth.
In the early years in Taiwan, Mr. Li operated two electroplating
factories, specializing in plating support for Nancy’s product business.
His deep technical knowledge and practical experience in plating
provided strong support to Kaigui’s manufacturing foundation.
As the industry evolved and the need for larger production space and
upgraded manufacturing capabilities became increasingly important, Mr.
Lee and Nancy Jan made a major decision in the 1990s: they moved
their operations to mainland China and established a factory in Kunshan.
This marked the beginning of what we now know as Kaigui Ornament’s
in-house plating and manufacturing operation.
Their partnership combined market experience, persistence, and
technical expertise. Together, they built Kaigui not only as a business,
but as a company rooted in craftsmanship, long-term thinking, and a
serious commitment to quality.
Today, Kaigui continues to carry forward the entrepreneurial spirit of
Nancy Jan and Mr. Lee — a spirit built on hard work, resilience, and the
belief that strong manufacturing should always be supported by honesty,
professionalism, and real industry knowledge.
