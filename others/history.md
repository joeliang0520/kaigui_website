Our History
About Kaigui
Kaigui Ornament Co., Ltd. is a metal ornament manufacturer built on a
strong foundation of industry experience. Our story began with the
previous generation of our family, whose work was deeply rooted in
metal finishing and related craftsmanship in Taiwan. Over the years, we
developed solid expertise in badges, coins, keychains, and various
custom metal gift products, with long-term experience in production,
quality control, and plating.
As the industry evolved, Kaigui gradually expanded its manufacturing
base to Kunshan, China in the 1990s. Located between Shanghai and
Suzhou, Kunshan is one of the key manufacturing hubs in the Yangtze
River Delta and is well known for its strict environmental standards,
manufacturing regulations, and supply chain efficiency. In this
environment, Kaigui continued to strengthen its production system and
management standards, growing into a professional factory known for
craftsmanship, consistency, and reliable lead times.
Built from Manufacturing, Not from Trading
What makes Kaigui different from a typical trading company is simple:
we are a factory. We understand every step of the manufacturing process
and what truly matters behind a good product—material selection,
stamping, Die casting, coloring, plating, packaging, quality control, and
the many small details that determine the final result.
In metal products especially, plating is not only about appearance. It also
affects durability, rust resistance, texture, and overall product quality.
Kaigui has its own plating-related qualifications and practical production
experience, which allows us to maintain stronger control over sampling,
mass production, and quality consistency without depending on multiple
outsourced parties. For our customers, this means clearer
communication, more stable quality, and a more controllable production
process.
More Than 50 Years of Industry Experience
Kaigui was not built overnight. What we carry forward is decades of
hands-on experience from the previous generation, along with a long-
standing commitment to craftsmanship. Over the years, we have served
customers in different countries and markets, producing badges,
challenge coins, medals, keychains, and a wide range of custom metal
products. Through long-term cooperation, we have developed a deep
understanding of quality expectations, responsibility, and on-time
delivery.
This experience also gives us a unique understanding of supply chains.
Because we come from the manufacturing side, we know how to
evaluate whether a factory is truly capable, quality-minded, and reliable
for long-term cooperation. For this reason, in addition to our own
production, Kaigui is also able to help customers connect with trusted
manufacturing resources in Asia, reducing the time, risk, and trial-and-
error often involved in supplier sourcing.
Looking Ahead
Today, Kaigui continues to move forward with manufacturing at its core,
integrity as its foundation, and long-term partnership as its goal. We
believe strong business relationships are built not only on price, but also
on professionalism, transparency, responsibility, and consistent
communication.
From one generation to the next, Kaigui represents more than a factory.
It represents an understanding of craftsmanship, a commitment to
quality, and a serious respect for every promise made to our customers.
Looking ahead, we will continue to provide reliable manufacturing and
supply chain support to customers around the world.
