A Message from the Second Generation
Hello, my name is Tom, and I am the second generation of Kaigui
Ornament Co., Ltd.
When I was only five months old, my parents brought me from Taiwan
to mainland China, where I grew up surrounded by the factory
environment. Since childhood, I have watched the machines run day
after day, and many of our outstanding employees are people I have
known since I was young. For me, Kaigui is not only a family business
— it is also a part of my childhood, my personal history, and my
understanding of how real work and real value are built over time.
Growing up in the factory taught me an important lesson: nothing
meaningful is achieved in a single step. Success comes from patience,
discipline, and the accumulation of small efforts over time. I see the
same principle in our products. If the artwork or mold is wrong at the
very beginning, then no matter how well the later steps are completed,
the final product will still not meet the right standard. This way of
thinking has shaped not only how I understand manufacturing, but also
how I understand responsibility, quality, and long-term business.
After graduating from the University of Toronto, OISE, with a Master’s
degree, I told my parents that I wanted to begin learning Kaigui’s
business and participate in trade shows. My decision came from both
passion and responsibility. I believe that after decades of hard work, my
parents have built a very strong foundation for Kaigui. My goal is to
continue building on that foundation, improve the company step by step,
and bring Kaigui to more potential markets around the world.
At the same time, I will continue to uphold the management values that
my parents have always believed in — especially the importance of
integrity, honesty, and responsible business practice. These values are
the reason Kaigui has come this far, and they will remain at the center of
how we serve both our existing customers and the new relationships we
hope to build in the future.
Most importantly, I want to sincerely thank everyone who has supported
Kaigui along the way. Without the trust of our customers, partners, and
long-term relationships, Kaigui would not be where it is today. We are
grateful for your continued support, and we look forward to serving you
with the same sincerity and dedication for many years to come.
